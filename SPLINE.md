# Spline Motion

Spline Motion is a two-person motion design agency focused on creating high-quality motion graphics and product videos for SaaS, technology, and B2B companies.

We help companies explain complex products, communicate new features, and launch products through strong visual storytelling and polished motion design.

Spline Motion is built by two friends:

**Barun Mazumder** - Co-founder & Motion Designer
Barun is an experienced motion designer with a strong focus on creating polished, engaging motion graphics and product-focused videos.

**Hriday Das** - Co-founder & Motion Designer
Hriday comes from a background in graphic design, web development, and UI design and is now specializing in motion design. His broader design and digital experience contributes to the visual and product-thinking side of Spline Motion.

## What Spline Motion Does

Spline Motion primarily works with SaaS, AI, technology, and B2B companies that need high-quality video content to communicate their products.

Our work can include:

* Product explainer videos
* Product launch videos
* Feature launch videos
* SaaS explainer videos
* Product demo videos
* Motion graphics
* UI/UX animation
* Brand motion
* Marketing and campaign videos
* Short-form product videos for social media

The focus is not simply on making things move. The goal is to understand what a product does, identify what needs to be communicated, and turn that information into a clear and visually compelling story.

## Our Positioning

Spline Motion is a specialized creative partner for technology companies that need strong motion design and product storytelling.

We are not trying to be a large traditional agency. We are a small, focused two-person studio where the people working directly on the project are the people building the company.

This allows us to stay close to the product, move quickly, communicate directly with clients, and maintain a high level of attention throughout the creative process.

The ambition is to build Spline Motion around exceptional motion design rather than simply offering video production as a commodity service.

## Our Approach

We care about both design and communication.

A technically impressive animation is not enough if the viewer does not understand the product. Likewise, a clear explanation should not feel visually generic.

Spline Motion aims to bring together:

**Strategy + Storytelling + Design + Motion**

The process starts with understanding the product and the audience, then finding the simplest and strongest way to communicate the idea visually.

The final work should feel intentional, modern, polished, and native to the technology space.

## Our Current Focus

Spline Motion is in its early stage and is focused on building strong relationships with SaaS and technology companies.

The immediate goal is to work with companies on real product and launch projects, build a strong body of work, and grow through the quality of that work and long-term client relationships.

We are particularly interested in products that are technically interesting but difficult to explain quickly. This includes new features, AI products, developer tools, APIs, infrastructure products, SaaS platforms, and other B2B technology.
